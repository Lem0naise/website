I Think We're Alone Now - Tiffany

Holdin' onto one another's hand

Tryin' to get away into the night / And then you put your arms around me

As we tumble to the ground and then you say / I think we're alone now

You know when people say the honeymoon period ends after three months? That's not necessarily a bad thing. Your brain literally builds a tolerance to the chemicals that give you that feeling, so after about nine months you will biologically feel it less. Relationships require effort to maintain, of course, crazy thought, but don't put all the pressure on yourself. If you put the same time and love into your relationship that you did when you were first dating, or even trying to date, you'd start to feel that childlike wonder and attraction again. It wouldn't feel quite the same, but you want to feel different feelings. It does require effort from both parties, but with secure love, you can add some whimsy and fun on top of a satisfyingly domestic relationship. The honeymoon period transitioning into a secure, safe relationship is a completely positive thing, provided you keep choosing your special person, even on the boring days.

Lean on Me - Club Nouveau

For no one can fill

Those of your needs / That you won't let show

Lean on me

I just watched San Junipero, and wow, what a heavy piece of television. I was expecting a typical Black Mirror dystopia and was hit with so much nostalgia, sadness, pain, pity, excitement, and, most prominently, the urge to pre-grieve a life I have not yet lived. When you love someone deeply, the brain tries to practise losing them as a defence mechanism. All this does is steal the joy out of the present moment and drain your energy for a hypothetical future that is not yet happening. San Junipero is asking whether it is better to live in a controlled, nostalgic fantasy where you never have to face loss, or to accept the impermanence of real life. I think the takeaway is that you do not have to choose per se: love is precious because life moves forward and we cannot freeze it in time. It is precious because we have chosen to dedicate time from our inherently limited lives to this fragile, risky, emotional pursuit of love.
