Stop trying to force yourself to be a morning person. If you just thought "why", then you're exactly who I'm talking to.

I've never been a morning person. I've tried, I promise. 

Here's some alternatives to that 6am alarm. 

### Have a good morning routine

This doesn't mean waking up at sunrise. However, it does involve not reaching for your phone immediately. My preferred routine nowadays is immediately taking a trip to the bathroom, which both requires you to keep your eyes open, and gets blood flowing through your legs. 

I then go back to my bed, prop a pillow up, and get my e-reader out and start reading! It doesn't have to be anything heavy or philosophical, even a comic book will do, but the effect on your brain of a calming, non-stimulating morning experience will do wonders.
