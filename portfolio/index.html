I've been building CashCat, a personal budgeting app where you can import multiple bank accounts, track your day-to-day spending, and set budgets.

I built the Climate Stories Library's website, hosting climate change videos from around the world plotted on an interactive world map.

As web officer for Southampton's Electronics and Computer Science Society, I worked on the ECSS website, hosting all our events, elections, merch, and member tools.

Electland is a multi-party local election game I made where you campaign in a fictional English town's council election.

Head and Heart is my minimal alternative to Letterboxd and Goodreads, where you can rate media in 2D. I wrote about it here.

The Political Playground is a national election simulation game I made with dozens of real-world countries and parties. I wrote a blog post about it.

Allegedly News is a newspaper generation website where you can simulate an alternative history scenario.

For Global Game Jam 2026 I made Damaskus with my friends: a desert-themed puzzle game. I wrote about the jam here.

Fantasteroids was the first project I finished. It's an Asteroids-style arcade game, free to play on Steam! There's a writeup here.

Mycelium Market was a hackathon project where wind, soil moisture, and rainfall drive a stock market on a world map.

The Kresimiria Wiki is a fictional worldbuilding wiki with laws, politicians, and more, styled to look like Wikipedia. Here is how it began.
